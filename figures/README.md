- Figure_1_circular_genome_comparison.png` - Circular genome comparison between *Bacillus nitratireducens* LABIM53 and 12 *B. nitratireducens* genomes from GenBank datasets. The LABIM53 reference is shown in grey, with predicted ORFs indicated in dark green. The inner plots display GC content (black) and GC skew (positive: cyan; negative: purple). The outer colored rings represent BLASTn alignments of each GenBank strain (DE0336, DE0363, DE0364, HD-50, JJ1048, N4150, NRS-319, 112MF, 4049, ATCC6463, BD-32, and BM02) against LABIM53; colored segments indicate regions of sequence similarity, whereas white gaps indicate absent or divergent regions.
- Figure_2_taxonomy_resolution.png` - Taxonomic resolution of *Bacillus nitratireducens* LABIM53 via ANY 16S rRNA phylogenetic analyses. (A) Whole-genome clustering heatmap based on OrthoANI values for *Bacillus* strains. Each cell shows the average nucleotide identity (ANI, %) between a pair of genomes. The color gradient ranges from green (lower ANI values) to red (higher ANI values), as indicated by the scale bar on the left. Strain names followed by "T" denote type or authentic strains. Node labels represent bootstrap support. The dendrograms above and to the left of the heatmap show hierarchical clustering based on ANI distances and are rooted in *B. subtilis* 168. (B) Phylogenetic analysis via Bayesian inference based on the 16S rRNA and gyrB gene sequences. Node labels indicate posterior probability values, and the scale bar represents the number of nucleotide substitutions per site. The *Bacillus nitratireducens* clade, including LABIM53, is highlighted in green. (C) Phylogenetic analysis via maximum likelihood based on the 16S rRNA and gyrB gene sequences. Node labels indicate bootstrap support values (%), and the scale bar represents the number of nucleotide substitutions per site. The Bacillus nitratireducens clade, including LABIM53, is highlighted in green.
- Figure_3_pangenome_and_COG.png` - Pangenome structure of *Bacillus nitratireducens* and COG functional classification of strain LABIM53. (A) Core-genome phylogenetic tree and presence/absence gene matrix for 14 *Bacillus strains* generated via ROARY. On the left, the tree was inferred from the core genome, and branch lengths are proportional to genetic distance. On the right, the binary matrix represents 12,044 gene clusters; each row corresponds to a strain, and each column corresponds to a gene cluster. Dark blue cells indicate the presence of a given gene cluster, and white cells indicate its absence. (B) COG functional classification of protein-coding genes in *Bacillus nitratireducens* LABIM53. The x-axis shows the functional categories encoded by single-letter COG codes, and the y-axis shows the number of sequences annotated in each category. Taller bars indicate greater representation of that function. Bar colors differentiate COG categories and do not represent quantitative differences. The letters follow the COG standard (J: Translation, ribosomal structure and biogenesis; A: RNA processing and modification; K: Transcription; L: Replication, recombination and repair; B: Chromatin structure and dynamics; D: Cell cycle control, cell division, chromosome partitioning; Y: Nuclear structure; V: Defense mechanisms; T: Signal transduction mechanisms; M: Cell wall/membrane/envelope biogenesis; N: Cell motility; Z: Cytoskeleton; W: Extracellular structures; U: Intracellular trafficking, secretion and vesicular transport; O: Posttranslational modification, protein turnover, chaperones; X: Mobilome: prophages, transposons; C: Energy production and conversion; G: Carbohydrate transport and metabolism; E: Amino acid transport and metabolism; F: Nucleotide transport and metabolism; H: Coenzyme transport and metabolism; I: Lipid transport and metabolism; P: Inorganic ion transport and metabolism; Q: Secondary metabolite biosynthesis, transport and catabolism; R: General function prediction only; S: Function unknown).
- Figure_4_nutrient_solubilisation.png` - Potassium (A) and phosphate (B) solubilisation by *Bacillus nitratireducens* LABIM53 on solid media grown for 30 days at 28ºC. Plate A shows colonies grown on Aleksandrov (ALEK) medium containing an insoluble potassium source, and plate B shows colonies grown on NBRIP medium containing an insoluble phosphate source. In both panels, translucent halo zones surrounding the colonies indicate solubilisation of the respective nutrient. Each plate displays duplicate inoculation locations.
