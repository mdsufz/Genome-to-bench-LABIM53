- Figure_1_circular_genome_comparison.png` - Circular genome comparison between *Bacillus nitratireducens* LABIM53 and 12 *B. nitratireducens* genomes from GenBank datasets. The LABIM53 reference is shown in grey, with predicted ORFs indicated in dark green. The inner plots display GC content (black) and GC skew (positive: cyan; negative: purple). The outer colored rings represent BLASTn alignments of each GenBank strain (DE0336, DE0363, DE0364, HD-50, JJ1048, N4150, NRS-319, 112MF, 4049, ATCC6463, BD-32, and BM02) against LABIM53; colored segments indicate regions of sequence similarity, whereas white gaps indicate absent or divergent regions.
- Figure_2_taxonomy_resolution.png` - Taxonomic resolution of *Bacillus nitratireducens* LABIM53 via ANY 16S rRNA phylogenetic analyses. (A) Whole-genome clustering heatmap based on OrthoANI values for *Bacillus* strains. Each cell shows the average nucleotide identity (ANI, %) between a pair of genomes. The color gradient ranges from green (lower ANI values) to red (higher ANI values), as indicated by the scale bar on the left. Strain names followed by "T" denote type or authentic strains. Node labels represent bootstrap support. The dendrograms above and to the left of the heatmap show hierarchical clustering based on ANI distances and are rooted in *B. subtilis* 168. (B) Phylogenetic analysis via Bayesian inference based on the 16S rRNA and gyrB gene sequences. Node labels indicate posterior probability values, and the scale bar represents the number of nucleotide substitutions per site. The *Bacillus nitratireducens* clade, including LABIM53, is highlighted in green. (C) Phylogenetic analysis via maximum likelihood based on the 16S rRNA and gyrB gene sequences. Node labels indicate bootstrap support values (%), and the scale bar represents the number of nucleotide substitutions per site. The Bacillus nitratireducens clade, including LABIM53, is highlighted in green.
- Figure_3_pangenome_and_COG.png` - (a) Core-genome phylogenetic tree and gene presence/absence matrix for 14 B. nitratireducens strains. The phylogenetic tree (left) was inferred using FastTree from the concatenated core-gene alignment generated by Roary and midpoint-rooted for visualisation; branch lengths represent substitutions per site. The binary matrix (right) comprises 11,769 gene clusters, with each row representing a strain and each column representing a gene cluster. Dark blue cells indicate the presence of a gene cluster, whereas white cells indicate its absence. (b) COG functional classification of protein-coding genes in B. nitratireducens LABIM53. The classification includes 4,348 proteins; the remaining 1,370 proteins without COG assignments are not shown. The x-axis displays the functional categories represented by single-letter COG codes, and the y-axis indicates the number of sequences assigned to each category. Taller bars indicate greater representation of the corresponding functional category. Bar colours distinguish COG categories and do not encode quantitative differences. The letters correspond to the standard COG categories (J: Translation, ribosomal structure and biogenesis; A: RNA processing and modification; K: Transcription; L: Replication, recombination and repair; B: Chromatin structure and dynamics; D: Cell cycle control, cell division, chromosome partitioning; Y: Nuclear structure; V: Defence mechanisms; T: Signal transduction mechanisms; M: Cell wall/membrane/envelope biogenesis; N: Cell motility; Z: Cytoskeleton; W: Extracellular structures; U: Intracellular trafficking, secretion and vesicular transport; O: Posttranslational modification, protein turnover, chaperones; X: Mobilome: prophages, transposons; C: Energy production and conversion; G: Carbohydrate transport and metabolism; E: Amino acid transport and metabolism; F: Nucleotide transport and metabolism; H: Coenzyme transport and metabolism; I: Lipid transport and metabolism; P: Inorganic ion transport and metabolism; Q: Secondary metabolite biosynthesis, transport and catabolism; R: General function prediction only; S: Function unknown). (c) Prokka-predicted products of the 179 CDSs unique to LABIM53, identified from the Roary gene presence/absence matrix. The x-axis displays numbered product groups corresponding to the adjacent legend, while the y-axis indicates the number of unique CDSs assigned to each group. Blue bars represent the 18 most frequent named products, the light-blue bar groups the remaining 10 distinct named products (10 CDSs), and the grey bar represents hypothetical proteins (148 CDSs). Numbers above the bars indicate CDS counts. Gene exclusivity is defined relative to the 14 genomes compared under the applied clustering parameters, and product annotations represent computational predictions.
- Figure_4_nutrient_solubilisation.png` - Potassium (A) and phosphate (B) solubilisation by *Bacillus nitratireducens* LABIM53 on solid media grown for 30 days at 28ºC. Plate A shows colonies grown on Aleksandrov (ALEK) medium containing an insoluble potassium source, and plate B shows colonies grown on NBRIP medium containing an insoluble phosphate source. In both panels, translucent halo zones surrounding the colonies indicate solubilisation of the respective nutrient. Each plate displays duplicate inoculation locations.
